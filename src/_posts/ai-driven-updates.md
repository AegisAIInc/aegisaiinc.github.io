The arms race between attackers and defenders is as old as computing. But when both sides use AI, the cycle accelerates. Static rules lose in days. Manual updates can't keep pace. AegisAI's answer: AI-generated heuristics that evolve as fast as the threats do.

## How Heuristic Generation Works

When AegisAI detects a novel threat pattern, it doesn't just log it. Our AI Update Generator analyzes the incident, simulates variations (what if the agent modified this behavior? what if it targeted a different subsystem?), and produces candidate heuristics. These candidates are validated in sandboxed environments—we don't deploy blind. Only heuristics that reliably detect the threat without excessive false positives make it into production.

This process runs continuously. Every confirmed incident feeds the generator. Reinforcement learning tunes the heuristics over time. The result: new defensive capabilities emerge within hours of a threat's first appearance, not weeks or months.

## Beating the Arms Race

Adversaries can probe our systems too. They can learn what triggers alerts and adapt. The difference is speed. Human-led development cycles in security take months. AegisAI's generative pipeline operates on a different timescale. By the time an attacker has iterated once, we've deployed multiple heuristic variants. The defense stays ahead because it's automated.

## What This Means for You

Pro and Enterprise users receive these AI-driven updates automatically. No patches to apply, no downtime. Your deployment gets smarter with every incident—yours or anyone else's in our federated network. That's the power of generative defense: the more the system sees, the stronger it becomes.
