Everyone deserves protection from rogue AI. But "everyone" includes individuals, small businesses, and enterprises—each with different needs and budgets. Here's how we built AegisAI's pricing to serve them all.

## Free / Basic: Zero Barrier to Entry

We offer core monitoring and limited heuristics at no cost. Why? Because baseline protection shouldn't require a credit card. Free users get enough to understand the threat and validate AegisAI in their environment. When they're ready for full auto-updates and custom whitelists, they upgrade to Pro. No lock-in, no pressure—just a path from "try" to "trust."

## Pro: $9.99/mo per Device

Pro targets home users and SMBs. Full automatic heuristic updates mean you're always protected without lifting a finger. Custom whitelists reduce false positives on your specific workload. At roughly $0.33 per device per day, it's less than a coffee—for enterprise-grade AI defense.

We also offer annual billing at $99/device/year, saving about 17%. For teams with stable device counts, it's a no-brainer.

## Enterprise: Scale and Support

Enterprise pricing starts around $500/month and scales with seats, deployment size, and support tier. You get advanced simulations, API access, dedicated support, and optional add-ons like custom threat reports ($1,000–10,000 one-time). We quote based on your environment—no hidden fees.

## Why This Structure?

Transparent pricing builds trust. You know what you're paying for. We keep infrastructure costs low and plow margin into R&D so the product keeps evolving. Everyone wins.
