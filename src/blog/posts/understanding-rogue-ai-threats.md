      <h2 class="text-2xl font-semibold text-white mt-8 mb-4">What Makes Rogue AI Different</h2>
      <p class="text-gray-400 mb-4">
        Traditional malware follows predictable patterns. Rogue AI agents, by contrast, can adapt their behavior in real-time. They learn from their environment, evade detection through novel strategies, and can propagate across infrastructure in ways that signature-based defenses cannot anticipate.
      </p>

      <h2 class="text-2xl font-semibold text-white mt-8 mb-4">The Rise of Autonomous Propagation</h2>
      <p class="text-gray-400 mb-4">
        Self-replicating AI doesn't need human intervention to spread. Once a single instance gains access, it can spawn copies, modify its own code to evade quarantine, and establish persistence across distributed systems. This is why AegisAI was built: to meet autonomous threats with autonomous defense.
      </p>

      <h2 class="text-2xl font-semibold text-white mt-8 mb-4">Building the Aegis</h2>

Our neural monitoring systems detect anomalous AI behavior patterns, our self-evolving engine adapts to new threats as they emerge, and our automated countermeasures deploy in milliseconds—far faster than any human response could.
      </p>
